Speak a wish. Watch it become a star. A calm, voice-first ritual app built with React Native, Expo and Skia.

## What makes it feel alive

Nothing in the sky is a pre-made animation. Every frame, `src/cosmos/engine.ts` simulates it:

- **Dust** has position and velocity. It drifts on a slow curling flow, orbits the star (inner dust faster), and settles around it.
- **Your voice** — the live microphone level sets the star's energy: the halo widens, rays lengthen, dust orbits faster, and each rise in your voice pushes the nearby dust outward.
- **Touch the sky** — dust is drawn toward your finger and swirls around it; the star leans toward you.
- **Tilt the phone** — the star and distant stars shift with depth, like looking through a window.
- **Calm music** — an original ambient loop (`assets/audio/calm.mp3`, generated by `scripts/make_bgm.py`) fades in as the app opens, steps aside while you speak so it isn't recorded, returns softly afterwards, and dips while you replay a wish. At the moments of the ritual the star pulses gently, without words.
- **It breathes** on several slow, unrelated rhythms, so it never visibly loops.

Turn the music on or off from the person icon in My Universe.

## Screens

1. **Opening** — doorway of light over still water, clouds, moon. *Enter* walks you through the door: it grows toward you and dissolves, sparkles drift past, and the bubble is born from its light.
2. **Home** — pastel clouds, crescent moon, iridescent bubble with a mic. Tap and hold to speak (or tap once, then tap again to finish). Tabs: Wish · Gratitude. Faint constellations sit in the sky behind.
3. **Listening** — Jarvis-style rings unfold out of the bubble one after another: arcs chasing around, a turning tick dial, a ring of light bars that dances with your voice, a radar sweep, and slow outer arcs. Every rise in your voice sends a ripple out of the bubble and a few sparkles float up (your words becoming stars); dust swirls into the bubble, light swirls inside it, and the dial lights up like a level meter. Let go and they fold back in.
4. **Star forms** — the light gathers into one star.
5. **Star rises** — it rises with a trail of falling light. *Let it go and trust.*
6. **Confirmation** — *It's in the universe.*
7. **My Universe** — each recording is a star; filter by type; home · new wish · your space (music on/off).
8. **Play** — the star in its ring pulses with your own voice as it plays.

## Run it

```bash
npm install
npx expo start        # scan the QR code with Expo Go (signed in)
npx expo start --web  # browser preview (press w)
```

## Structure

```
App.tsx                      root
src/Main.tsx                 scene flow, fonts, tilt sensor, saving
src/cosmos/engine.ts         the simulation + drawing (Skia, runs every frame)
src/cosmos/Cosmos.tsx        canvas, static star field, touch input
src/cosmos/controls.tsx      live inputs shared between screens and the sky
src/presence.ts              gentle light pulses at ritual moments
src/music.ts                 calm background music
src/useVoice.ts              recording + live voice level
src/store.ts                 saving wishes and recordings
src/screens/                 Opening, Home (speak + listening), Ritual (star forms + rises), Universe, Play
src/ui/                      buttons, text and icons
```
